# Public release scope

Version 1.0.0, prepared 26 October 2026, accompanies the revised BSPC-D-25-08366 manuscript.

Eighteen assets are byte-identical copies of the selected submission archives. Two assets are explicitly named public subsets of the original XAI Location Prior Sensitivity and Seed Pair Agreement packages. MANIFEST.json gives both the public and original hashes and lists every omitted member. Retained original members are byte-identical.

The location-prior subset omits the compact spatial-input NPZ and aggregate spatial-atlas NPZ. The seed-pair subset omits 98 INbreast binary-domain arrays. Redistribution authority for these spatial derivatives has not been established. This is not a finding that sharing is prohibited. Author-generated numerical summaries, code, saved predictions and signed model responses remain available where present in the original packages. Complete spatial replay requires the omitted inputs. Complete original archives remain preserved in the journal submission materials.

Original and processed mammogram intensity images, visual case atlases, fitted or pretrained weights, manuscript documents and reviewer correspondence are outside this public release. Their absence does not remove configurations or unfavorable results from the numerical benchmark.

Some immutable records describe an earlier local or editorial-only package, pending execution or older reporting conventions. Those descriptions are dated provenance. This release notice establishes the present publication status of the listed assets without retroactively changing their experimental records. RESULTS_GUIDE.md identifies the current reporting precedence.

Historical local paths are retained as inert provenance strings. Portable replay adapters use package-relative inputs. Historical controllers remain inspection-only. Third-party notices accompany the release and no new blanket license is imposed. No new scientific calculations, training or inference were performed for this publication.
